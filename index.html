About me

Who I am

I'm an electronics engineer (BIT Mesra) turned robotics student. At Samsung I owned firmware releases end to end, from sanitizer runs and memory profiling on new hardware to the final factory image, and I built tools so my team could stop doing slow manual work.

Now at Manchester I'm moving that discipline onto robots: getting real hardware to map, perceive and act reliably.

Career goals & research interests

Goal: a robotics software role after my MSc, as a robotics software, SLAM, or test and simulation engineer, where my release and debugging background is a real advantage.

  • SLAM that stays reliable when lighting changes
  • Perception with RGB and depth cameras for grasping
  • Gaussian-splatting maps and simulation for robot learning
  • Reinforcement learning for decision-making on real hardware

Samsung R&D · Python

Factory firmware upload automation

An OCR, OpenCV and PyAutoGUI tool that fills in the factory registration system by itself, plus a separate checker that confirms every image by checksum. A two-hour, four-person job became one operator. It was adopted across the TV Product Group, and I led three interns extending it.

PythonOpenCVOCRPyAutoGUIChecksums
Samsung R&D · Java

Defect-tracker bulk creator

The stock defect tool crashed after 10–15 entries, so linked fixes across many files took hours. I wrote a Java client on the APIs supplied by Samsung HQ that creates 40–200 linked child records in seconds, with project, date and developer filled in automatically.

JavaAPI integrationRelease tooling
Independent research In progress

Does SLAM survive the lights going down?

A study of how SLAM accuracy changes from bright to dim lighting, using my TortoiseBot and slam_toolbox. The plan is to measure trajectory error (ATE/RPE) with evo, with five runs for each lighting level to get error bars.

slam_toolboxevoExperiment design
Research · ESI 2025, Qatar University

Hybrid ML + decision-making ranking

Combined Random Forest and Gradient Boosting with three ranking methods (VIKOR, MOORA, EDAS) to rank Jharkhand districts for sustainable mining. All three methods gave similar rankings, which supports the result. I presented this in Doha.
